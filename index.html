Privacy Policy for NoteApt

Questions? Contact us at lotofapps@gmail.com

In short: Your notes stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, they are backed up to your own Google Drive. We run no servers, have no user accounts, and cannot see your notes. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the NoteApt app ("the App"), developed by LotOfApps ("we", "us"), handles information. We do not operate any server that stores your notes or personal data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to your notes, attachments, or personal information on any server of ours. The App has no accounts of its own and uses no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Everything you create (text, checklists, images, audio recordings, videos, tags, categories, reminders and settings) is stored locally on your device.

3. Google sign-in and Google Drive sync (optional)

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to back up and sync your notes.

  • What the App requests: your basic Google profile (email address, name and profile picture) and access to files that the App itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The App cannot see your other Drive files.
  • What it stores in your Drive: a folder named MyNotes App Data containing your synced notes, photos, audio recordings, videos you choose to upload, and backup files.
  • Your profile details are kept only on your device so the App can show which account is signed in.
  • Who can see it: only you. Your synced data stays in your Google account. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

You can turn sync off for any note, sign out, or delete the "MyNotes App Data" folder in Google Drive at any time. You can also revoke the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Note locking and encryption

If you lock a note with a PIN, the text of that note is encrypted on your device (AES, with a key derived from your PIN). Your PIN is never sent to us. Only a hashed form of it is kept on your device. If a locked note is synced, the encrypted text is what is uploaded to your Drive.

Attached photos, audio recordings and videos are not encrypted by the App, including when they are backed up to your Drive or exported. Please keep this in mind when adding sensitive media.

5. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including a short full-screen ad that may appear when you launch the App. AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app interaction data, to deliver ads and measure their performance. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing ads.

6. Permissions

The App may ask for the following permissions. Each is used only for the feature described.

  • Microphone: voice typing and audio recording.
  • Camera: taking photos and videos to attach to notes.
  • Photos, videos and files: attaching media you choose, and saving or restoring backups.
  • Notifications: reminders you set.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: App Lock.
  • Internet: Google Drive sync and ads.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback.

7. Voice typing and read-aloud

Voice typing uses your device's speech recognition service, provided by Google or your device manufacturer. Depending on your device and language, your speech may be processed on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive or store this audio. Read-aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties, except that Google AdMob receives the advertising-related data described in section 5, and Google processes your sign-in and Drive sync as described in section 3. When you export or share a note (image, PDF, text, media or note file), it goes where you choose, using your device's share options.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Signing out does not delete your notes from your device.
  • In Google Drive: synced files stay in your Drive until you remove them. Inside the App you can choose "Remove from Drive" for a note, or delete the "MyNotes App Data" folder yourself in Google Drive. Uninstalling the App does not delete files already in your Drive.
  • With us: we hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email lotofapps@gmail.com.
